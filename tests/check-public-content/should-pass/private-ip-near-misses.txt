The near-miss controls for the RFC1918 block patterns. Every address here is
outside private space or is not an address at all, so a pattern that widened
past its block fails this fixture rather than teaching people to route around
a noisy gate.
The documentation ranges belong in examples: 192.0.2.10, 198.51.100.7 and
203.0.113.9, per RFC 5737.
Just outside 172.16/12, both public space: 172.15.0.1 and 172.32.0.1.
Just outside 10/8, both public space: 9.255.255.255 and 11.0.0.1.
Just outside 192.168/16, both public space: 191.168.1.1 and 192.169.1.1.
Public resolvers stay legal: 1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8 and 9.9.9.9.
A longer dotted sequence is not an address: 1.10.0.0.1 and 10.0.0.1.5.
An identifier that merely starts with digits is not an address: v10.0.0.1.
Version prose is not an address: LFS 13.0 and the 3.14 interpreter.
